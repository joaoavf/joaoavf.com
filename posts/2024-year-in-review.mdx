### Intro
2024 was a pivotal year for me and for [Picnic](http://usepicnic.com/). It was the year that Picnic scaled from a scrappy MVP to a full-fledged product with over 10k users that rely on us to handle their onchain finances. 

<Image src="picnic-volume-2024.png" alt="Picnic volume in 2024" />

It became clear that making onchain easy has a lot of value and that Picnic is uniquely positioned to deliver that value, as we are [the early category leader](https://dune.com/picnic/picnic). 

<Image src="2024-market-share.png" alt="ERC 4337 swap market share" />

### Picnic launch
It is weird to look back and realize that it was only in January 2024 that we launched the Picnic Safe with ERC4337. And although our previous MVP provided a similar user experience on paper, it was much more convoluted and in consequence much harder for users to understand and trust. 

Migrating to the correct architecture was not a clear decision, because we didn't see it immediately affecting topline metrics, but I think setting up the foundations correctly was one of our best decisions and it has been compounding ever since. 

### Partnership with Defiverso
In March, we met [Lucas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUlyaQGFGag) back in March and we had an immediate bond, he is an individual I admire, charismatic and principled, who instantly understood what we were building and where we wanted to go. 

Lucas and the Defiverso community were pivotal to bring awareness to Picnic as well as explain it to a much wider audience, bringing 10x growth over the course of a few weeks and changing the course of our trajectory.

<Image src="lucas-picnic-screenshot.png" alt="Lucas first video about Picnic, how to buy crypto the right way" />

### Scaling pains 
The sudden growth was amazing and gave us a huge boost as a company, but suddendly a lot of technical debt became apparent. We spent April and May solving issues and establishing a customer success discipline, which was pivotal for our continued success.

<Image src="it-only-gets-worse.jpg" alt="Nikita Bier it only gets worse meme" width={500}/>

### Cross-chain Picnic
In June, once our business as usual was more under control, we expanded Picnic into 9 different chains and thousand of assets. It was amazing to see that we solved a substantial amount of our users requests and has been growing in share every month since the launch.

<Image src="crosschain-share.png" alt="Picnic cross-chain share has been steadily increasing since launch" />

### Fundraising
Despite growing quite a lot and having a very promissing product in hand and in desperate need of resources so we could continue in our journey. Fundraising was extremely hard to achieve. 

I spent a lot of time refining our pitch and connecting with different folks. This proved to be extremely challenging, but eventually we found amazing investors that deeply grasped what we were building and that were excited about our vision of making DeFi easy and turning that into the financial rails of the world.

### Europe launch
We went live in Europe in September, offering an IBAN to our European users via [Monerium](https://monerium.com/), which meant that could handle day to day finances and payments inside Picnic. This is a feature I am particularly bullish on, because it is an initial demo of what DeFi can become once it gets more mature.

<Image src="iban-demo.png" alt="Picnic UI displaying an IBAN number for deposit (mocked data)"  width={360} />

### Europe trip
In September, I went to Europe and was amazing to connect in person with folks from CV Labs, Safe and Gnosis (among many others). These individuals were extremely important for our journey and help establish the bridges that enabled us to continue building our vision.

### Devcon
In November, I went to Asia for the first time in my life for Devcon Bangkok. And it was one of the most meaningful experiences of my life. I was able to [speak in the main event and tell our story and how Picnic is making DeFi acessible](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKbsZkxeJNs). 

<Image src="devcon-talk-screenshot.png" alt="My talk at Devcon Bangkok in 2024" />

It became clear that we are global leaders in our category, simple things like using stablecoins for on/off ramp are still non-consensus to the market. So it is great to see that our ideas are paying off and that we have a much deeper understanding than most market participants.

### Looking ahead
I am extremely bullish on Picnic. I think we can grow 10x-100x over 2025. We need to crush the execution in product and distribution, mut all the fundamentals are now in place: super strong team in all key disciplines and the right partnerships and resources to make it happen.
