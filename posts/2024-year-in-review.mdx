### Intro
2024 was a pivotal year for me and for Picnic. It was the year that Picnic scaled from a scrappy MVP to a full-fledged product with over 10k users that rely on us to handle their onchain finances. 

It became clear that making onchain easy had a lot of value and that there are many opportunities for Picnic to fill better than current alternatives.

### Picnic launch
It is weird to look back and realize that it was only in January 2024 that we launched the Picnic Safe with ERC4337. And although our previous MVP provided a similar user experience, it was much more convoluted and in consequence much harder for users to understand and trust. 
Migrating to the correct architecture was not a super clear business decision, because we didn't see it immediately affecting topline metrics, but I think setting up the foundations correctly was one of our best decisions and the dividends of that decisions have been compounding ever since. 

### Partnership with Defiverso
In March, we met Lucas back in March and we had an immediate connection, he is an individual I admire, charismatic and principled, who instantly understood what we were building and where we wanted to go. He was pivotal to bring awareness to Picnic as well as explain it to a much wider audience, bringing 10x growth over the course of a few weeks and changing the course of our trajectory.

### Scaling pains 
The sudden growth was amazing and gave us a huge boost as a company, but suddendly a lot of improvements and technical debt became more apparent. We spent April and May solving technical debt and establishing a customer success discipline, which was pivotal for our continued success.

### Cross-chain Picnic
In June, once our business as usual was more under control, we expanded Picnic into 9 different chains and thousand of assets. It was amazing to see that we solved a substantial amount of our users requests and has been growing in importance on our portfolio every month since the launch.

### Fundraising
Despite growing quite a lot and having a very promissing product in hand and in desperate need of resources so we could continue in our journey. Fundraising was extremely hard to achieve. I spent a lot of time refining our pitch and connecting with different folks. This proved to be extremely challenging, but eventually we found amazing investors that deeply grasped what we were building and that were excited about our vision of making DeFi acessible and turning that into the financial rails of the world.

### Europe launch
We went live in a new geography in September, offering an IBAN to our European users via Monerium, which meant that could handle day to day finances and payments inside Picnic. Being the very first demo of a thesis I am deeply bullish on, which is that DeFi will be more efficient than TradFi.

### Europe trip
In September, I went to Europe and was amazing to connect in person with folks from CV Labs, Safe and Gnosis (among many others). These individuals were extremely important for our journey and help establish the bridges that enabled us to continue building our vision.

### Devcon
In November, I went to Asia for the first time in my life for Devcon Bangkok. And it was one of the most meaningful experiences of my life. I was able to speak in the main event and tell our story and how Picnic is making DeFi acessible.

### Looking ahead
I am extremely optimistic about Picnic in 2025, I think we have the main building blocks in place so we can grow 10x-100x over the year. Now we need to be heads down shipping while spreading our message.
